Zucchini Frutti di Mare Stuffed Bites
Ingredients: zucchini, tomato sauce, olive oil, parmesan cheese, salt, fresh parsley
Steps: Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Cut zucchini into halves lengthwise. Remove seeds from zucchini and stuff with a mixture of tomato sauce, olive oil, parmesan cheese, salt, and finely chopped fresh parsley. Place stuffed zucchini in a baking dish lined with parchment paper. Bake for 25-30 minutes until golden brown on top. Serve hot as an appetizer. Garnish with additional fresh parsley if desired.

Aceto Balsamico Risotto
Ingredients: Rice Arborio, Vegetable broth, White wine, Garlic cloves, Parmesan cheese, Basil leaves, Aceto balsamico condimento
Steps: Heat broth and wine in a saucepan over medium heat until simmering. In a large skillet, sauté garlic until tender but not brown. Add rice and sauté for 2 minutes. Pour in broth mixture, reduce heat to low, cover, and cook until most of the liquid has been absorbed. Repeat process adding more broth as needed until the rice is tender and creamy. Fold in chopped basil and parmesan cheese. Drizzle with balsamic glaze just before serving. Let risotto rest for a few minutes before serving.

Capricciosa Pane Breakfast Pizza
Ingredients: Whole Wheat Gluten-Free Bread Crust, Mixed Fresh Salami Slices, Cured Prosciutto, Grilled Tomatoes, Red Onion Rings, Arugula
Steps: Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C) and lightly grease a baking sheet. Slice bread into thin rounds and place on the baking sheet. Place slices of fresh salami and prosciutto on each round evenly. Drizzle each piece with olive oil and sprinkle with a pinch of sea salt. Roast in the preheated oven for about 10 minutes or until the crust is crispy and lightly browned. While pizzas are baking, halve and grill tomatoes over medium heat until tender. Slice grilled tomatoes and distribute over each pizza. Arrange red onion rings around the pizza rounds. Fold arugula leaves and serve immediately. Optional: drizzle with balsamic glaze before serving.

Tiramisu
Ingredients: Almond Cake Cubes, Coffee-soaked Ladyfingers, Heavy Cream
Steps: Preheat your oven to 175°C (350°F). Grease and flour four small loaf pans. In a mixing bowl, beat together eggs and sugar until light and fluffy. Fold in almond cake cubes gently. Tiramisu
Ingredients: gelato, coffee-brown sugar, unsweetened cocoa powder, egg yolks, caster sugar, whipped cream
Steps: Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease a 9x13 inch baking dish. In a blender, combine gelato, coffee-brown sugar, unsweetened cocoa powder, egg yolks, and caster sugar. Blend until smooth. Pour mixture into prepared dish. Bake for 20 minutes or until set on top. While cake is baking, prepare whipped cream by whipping cream until stiff peaks form. Top cooled cake with whipped cream. Serve chilled.

Peach Melba Tartlets
Ingredients: Peaches, Basil Leaves, Almond Flakes, Caster Sugar
Steps: Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Prepare peaches by removing the pits and cutting into slices. In a mixing bowl, blend caster sugar with 1 cup of water until the sugar is dissolved. Place peach slices in a baking dish, pour sugar syrup over them, and place in preheated oven for 5 minutes. Remove from oven and let it rest for a few minutes. Meanwhile, prepare tartlet cases by greasing muffin tins. Roll out almond flakes and press them into the prepared muffin tins. After the peaches cool, spoon them into the tarts. Garnish with fresh basil leaves. Re-bake in the oven at 375°F (190°C) for about 15 minutes. Serve cold as a refreshing dessert.

Lemon Mascarpone Tart with Candied Citrus Zest
Ingredients: Almond flour, Sugar, Butter, Eggs, Grated lemon zest, Sifted powdered sugar
Steps: Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease a tart pan. In a mixing bowl, cream the butter and sugar until light and fluffy. Add the eggs, one at a time, mixing well after each addition. Fold in the almond flour until just combined, being careful not to overmix. Stir in the grated lemon zest. Pour the mixture into the prepared tart pan. Bake for approximately 30 minutes, or until golden brown.

Steps: Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Slice eggplant into ½-inch thick rounds and lightly salt them. Allow to sit for 30 minutes to release excess moisture.
